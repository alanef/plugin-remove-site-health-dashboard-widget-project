* Fixed: on sites running other Fullworks free plugins, the opt-in notice could belong to the wrong plugin
* Fixed: deleting the plugin now also removes its opt-in setting
* Changed: the notice now links straight to the opt-in screen and no longer says you haven't visited settings
* Changed: requires PHP 7.4 or later; the bundled opt-in library has needed it since 1.0.1
* Changed: updated the bundled opt-in library to 1.3.3
